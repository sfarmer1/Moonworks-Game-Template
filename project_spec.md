# Project Spec

## Definition:
Product purpose, functionality and "jobs to be done"

## Answers:
- Who is the product for?
  - Indie game fans looking for a new classic to get obsessed with
- What problems does it solve?
  - Boredom
  - Apathy
  - Feels cozy
- What does the product do?
  - Entertains players of all ages
  - Teaches players how to play it
  - Consistently surprises and delights players
  - Creates unique, emotional, and shareable player-centric stories
  - Tells meaningful stories about LGBTQIA+ characters


## Design Acceptance Criteria
As a player, I should be able to...
- Spend 5-30 minutes and get lost on a fantastic journey
- Feel like I never know what I'm going to experience on each run
- Feel like improving my skills is always rewarded with persistent progress
- Feel like I've gained something from my time investment, even after a loss.
- Have fun that feels personally meaningful to me
- Feel like this experience has been handcrafted to appeal to a specific kind of person
- Feel like I'm breaking the game, but still be met with interesting challenges
- Make decisions that balance short term survival needs against long term happiness needs
  - e.g. Give my characters insane buffs at the cost of their authenticity

As a player, I should be able to...

### Setup
- Creatively assemble a team from a limited pool of talent

### Match
- Compete against teams of armed enemies
- Use character abilities in turn-based combat to complete objectives
- Level characters up until they can ascend
- Shepherd characters through a crucible so they can be find community and be transformed into something greater, not something less
- Long term consequences for failing characters is they are memorialized in-game.
  - Perhaps they are absorbed by the enemy?
- Jump in with sane defaults and just have fun exploring
- Feel like each character has a unique story to tell
- Feel like I'm exploring kalidescopic cyberspace worlds ala The Midnight Gospel
- Embody the spirit of the cyberspace Grim Reaper (AI?) 

- Squad Characters are IRL people who are playing an MMO
- Enemies are other PC's and NPC's
- Getting players through the crucible successfully means they get to earn the right to transform as long as their authenticity is at 100%
- Getting more players to the end, unlocks higher difficulties at specific thresholds
